# 🤝 How to help the roadmap

[Русский](CONTRIBUTING.md) · **English**

Thanks for stopping by. ML moves fast and I can't keep track of everything alone, so any help is genuinely useful.

## What helps the most

- **Dead and outdated links.** A course moved, a repo got archived, a tool nobody uses anymore. Links are checked automatically once a month, but human eyes still catch more.
- **Good resources that are missing.** Especially free ones, and especially in Russian.
- **Market experience.** If you've been interviewing recently and see that something here is off (they ask different things, the stack changed, salaries moved), tell me. That's the most valuable kind of feedback.
- **Typos and clumsy wording.** Also welcome.

## How to suggest something

**The easiest way** is to open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose). There are templates for "Suggest a resource" and "Error or dead link". A couple of lines is enough.

**If you want to fix it yourself**, send a PR:

1. Fork the repo and create a branch.
2. Update **both** versions, `docs/ru/` and `docs/en/`. If Russian is hard for you, update only the English one and say so in the PR, I'll do the rest.
3. Briefly explain why in the PR description. For example: "TGI was archived in 2026, replaced it with SGLang".
4. Working on ML Coach? It lives in `plugins/ml-roadmap/skills/ml-coach/`. Interview questions are in `references/questions.md`, system design cases in `references/interview.md`. Add new questions with a level (J/M/S) and the key points of a good answer.

## A few rules to keep things tidy

- **No pirated books.** No PDFs and no file-sharing links to paid books. Only official pages, and if the author published the book for free, a link to their site.
- **No ads.** Paid courses are fine if they're really strong and there's something to say about them, but no referral links.
- **Check a link before adding it.** Obvious, but half of all dead links start this way.
- **Less but better.** This is a roadmap, not an awesome list. If a section already has three good courses on a topic, a fourth one only makes sense if it's clearly better.
